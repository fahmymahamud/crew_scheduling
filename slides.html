The whole system

Three screens, once a week

STEP 1

Crew update availability

Each person opens one link on their phone and ticks the days they can work.

STEP 2

Office posts a job

Client, address, date, time, how many people, and what the job covers.

STEP 3

Assign crews & send

Tick the names the system says are free. It writes the WhatsApp message.

The three buttons along the top of the demo are exactly these three steps.

Step 1 · on the crew's phone

The crew tell you when they're free

  • Tick a day, set from and until. That's the whole form.
  • Add a remark — "must leave by 1pm" — and the office sees it next to the name.
  • Change it any time; the office view updates with it.
  • In the demo, use Pretend to be to switch between all ten crew members.

Step 2 · in the office

Post the job the moment it's booked

  • Tick the services included and the price totals itself as you go.
  • The job appears on the calendar straight away, marked as needing crew.

Step 3 · assigning

Pick the crew without cross-checking anything

  • Everyone is sorted into three groups for that specific job: free for the whole window, free that day but not all of it, or not available.
  • Each name carries the hours that person submitted.
  • The counter shows 2 of 3 until the job is filled.
  • Name a lead — they go first in the message, in square brackets.

Step 3 · sending

The message writes itself

  • Grouped by day, in job order, for the week you're looking at.
  • Lead first, in brackets. Then the rest of the crew, numbered.
  • Time, location and your note for the job, already filled in.
  • Edit anything in the box, press Copy, paste into the group.

When plans change

Changes take one click, not a rewrite

Client cancels

Press Cancel job. It stays on the calendar struck through, so you keep the record — and it drops out of the next message automatically.

Booked by mistake

Press Delete and confirm. The job and its crew assignments are gone.

Someone drops out

Untick the name. The job turns amber and the counter shows the shortfall until you fill it.

Nothing has to be retyped — regenerate the message and it reflects every change.

Your turn

Open the demo and click anything

  • Every button is live — create a job, assign a crew, generate the message.
  • Nothing is saved. Refresh, or press Start over, and the sample week returns.
  • The sample week is always the current week, so it never looks stale.
